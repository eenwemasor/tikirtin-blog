# Setting Up Your Organizer Profile

An organizer profile tells attendees who is behind the event. A complete and professional profile builds trust and helps you promote all your events in one place.

---

## 1. Go to Your Account Settings

1.  Log in to your Tikirtin account.
2.  Click on your profile icon in the top right corner and select **Account Settings** or **My Profile**.

---

## 2. Navigate to the Organizer Profile Section

1.  Find the **Organizer Profile** or **Organization** tab.
2.  If you don't have a profile yet, click **Create Organizer Profile**.

---

## 3. Fill in Your Profile Details

Provide information about your organization:

-   **Organizer Name**: The public name of your company or organization.
-   **Profile URL**: A custom URL for your public page (e.g., `tikirtin.com/my-org`).
-   **Bio/Description**: A short summary of what your organization does.
-   **Logo**: Upload your company logo.
-   **Contact Info**: Add a public email, website, and social media links.

---

## 4. Save Your Profile

1.  Click **Save Profile**.
2.  Your organizer profile is now live. All events you create will automatically be linked to this profile, and attendees can visit your page to see all your upcoming events.

---

## ✅ Tips

-   **Use a Clear Logo**: Your logo is a key part of your brand. Make sure it's high-quality.
-   **Write a Compelling Bio**: Explain what makes your events special.
-   **Keep it Updated**: If your contact information or social media links change, be sure to update your profile.

---

## 🚨 Troubleshooting

-   **Profile not saving?** Check that you have filled in all the required fields.
-   **URL not available?** Someone else may have already taken that custom URL. Try a different one.
-   **Logo looks blurry?** Make sure you are uploading a high-resolution image file.
