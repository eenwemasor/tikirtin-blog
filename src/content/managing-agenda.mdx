# Managing the Event Agenda

A well-structured agenda is key to a successful event. It guides attendees and ensures your event runs smoothly. This guide explains how to create and manage your event's agenda.

---

## 1. Go to Your Event Dashboard

1.  Log in to your Tikirtin account.
2.  Select **Events** from the main menu.
3.  Click on the event you wish to manage.

---

## 2. Access the Agenda Section

1.  From the event editor, navigate to the **Agenda** tab.
2.  Click **Add Agenda Item** to open the form.

---

## 3. Enter Agenda Details

Fill in the details for each agenda item:

- **Title** – The name of the session, e.g., _"Opening Keynote"_, _"Workshop: AI in Marketing"_.
- **Description** – A brief overview of what the session will cover.
- **Start & End Time** – The specific time slot for this item.
- **Applicable Dates** – Select which event date(s) this agenda item applies to.
- **Speaker (Optional)** – Assign a speaker to the session. You must add speakers under the **Speakers** tab first.

---

## 4. Save and Organize

1.  Click **Save Agenda Item**.
2.  Your new item will appear in the agenda list. You can drag and drop items to reorder them.
3.  The agenda will be displayed on your event page, helping attendees plan their day.

---

## ✅ Tips

- **Be Descriptive**: Clearly explain what each session is about to help attendees choose.
- **Include Breaks**: Don't forget to schedule breaks for networking, lunch, or rest.
- **Assign Speakers**: Linking speakers to sessions adds credibility and provides more context for attendees.

---

## 🚨 Troubleshooting

- **Agenda not appearing on the event page?** Make sure your event is published and you have saved the agenda items.
- **Can't assign a speaker?** Ensure the speaker has been added in the **Speakers** tab before creating the agenda item.
- **Times are incorrect?** Double-check that your event's timezone is set correctly in the **Settings** tab.
