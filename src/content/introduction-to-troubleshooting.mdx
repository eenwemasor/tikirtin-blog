# Customizing Your Event Page

Make your event page stand out by customizing its design and branding. This guide shows you how to change the theme, upload a banner, and adjust the layout.

---

## 1. Go to Your Event Dashboard

1.  Log in to your Tikirtin account.
2.  Select **Events** from the main menu.
3.  Click on the event you wish to customize.

---

## 2. Access the Customization Section

1.  From the event editor, navigate to the **Design** or **Branding** tab.
2.  Here, you will find options to modify the appearance of your event page.

---

## 3. Customize Your Page

You can typically modify the following:

-   **Theme Color**: Select a primary color that matches your brand.
-   **Banner Image**: Upload a high-resolution image (e.g., 1920x1080px) to be displayed at the top of your page.
-   **Logo**: Add your organization's logo.
-   **Layout**: Choose from different layouts for how sections like "Speakers," "Agenda," and "Tickets" are displayed.

---

## 4. Save and Publish

1.  Click **Save Changes**.
2.  Preview the page to see how it looks.
3.  Once you are happy with the design, ensure your event is published for the changes to be visible to attendees.

---

## ✅ Tips

-   **Use High-Quality Images**: A professional banner image can significantly improve the look of your page.
-   **Stay On-Brand**: Use colors and logos that are consistent with your organization's branding.
-   **Preview on Mobile**: Check how your page looks on a mobile device to ensure a good experience for all users.

---

## 🚨 Troubleshooting

-   **Images not displaying correctly?** Check the recommended dimensions and file formats (e.g., JPG, PNG).
-   **Colors not updating?** Clear your browser cache or try viewing the page in an incognito window.
-   **Changes not saved?** Make sure you are clicking the "Save" button before exiting the editor.

---
