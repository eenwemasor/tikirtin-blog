# Managing Event Speakers

Adding speakers to your event page helps build credibility and attract attendees. This guide shows you how to add, update, and manage speaker profiles.

---

## 1. Go to Your Event Dashboard

1.  Log in to your Tikirtin account.
2.  Select **Events** from the main menu.
3.  Click on the event you wish to manage.

---

## 2. Access the Speakers Section

1.  From the event editor, navigate to the **Speakers** tab.
2.  Click **Add Speaker** to open the speaker form.

---

## 3. Enter Speaker Details

Fill in the speaker's information:

- **Full Name** – The speaker's name as it should appear publicly.
- **Title/Role** – e.g., _CEO at Innovate Corp_, _Lead Developer_.
- **Bio** – A short paragraph about the speaker's expertise and background.
- **Profile Photo** – Upload a high-quality headshot.

Optional details:

- **Social Media Links** – Add links to their LinkedIn, Twitter, or personal website.
- **Assigned Sessions** – Link the speaker to specific agenda items or sessions they will be leading.

---

## 4. Save and Display

1.  Click **Save Speaker**.
2.  The speaker's profile will automatically appear on your event page in the "Speakers" section.
3.  You can edit or remove speakers at any time from the **Speakers** tab.

---

## ✅ Tips

- **Use Professional Headshots**: Clear, high-quality photos make your event look more professional.
- **Keep Bios Concise**: A brief, impactful bio is more effective than a long one.
- **Link to Relevant Sessions**: Connecting speakers to their sessions helps attendees plan their schedule.

---

## 🚨 Troubleshooting

- **Speaker not showing up?** Ensure you have saved the profile and that your event page is published.
- **Photo not uploading?** Check if the image file meets the size and format requirements (e.g., JPG, PNG, under 5MB).
- **Can't find a session to link?** Make sure you have created the agenda/session first under the **Agenda** tab.
