# Creating Tickets for Your Event

Creating tickets is the first step in making your event accessible to attendees. Follow these steps to set up different ticket types and prices.

---

## 1. Navigate to Your Event

1. Log in to your dashboard.
2. Select **Events** from the sidebar.
3. Click on the event you want to manage.

---

## 2. Open the Tickets Tab

1. In your event editor, click on the **Tickets** tab.
2. Press **Add Ticket**.

## 3. Configure Ticket Details

Fill in the required fields:

- **Ticket Name** – e.g. _General Admission_, _VIP Pass_.
- **Price** – Set the ticket cost (leave as `0` for free tickets).
- **Quantity Available** – Total number of tickets available.
- **Sales Dates** – Define when sales open and close.

Optional settings:

- **Description** – Add details like seating info or perks.
- **Access Code** – Restrict sales to users with a special code.
- **Ticket Limit** – Set a per-order limit (e.g., max 4 per person).

---

## 4. Save & Publish

1. Click **Save Ticket**.
2. To make it live, toggle **Publish Tickets**.
3. Your tickets will now appear on the event page.

---

## ✅ Tips

- Use **Early Bird Tickets** to encourage early sales.
- Always double-check **capacity** to avoid overselling.
- For complex pricing, create multiple ticket tiers (e.g., _Standard_, _VIP_, _Backstage Pass_).

---

## 🚨 Troubleshooting

- **Ticket not visible?** Make sure sales dates are active and the ticket is published.
- **Unable to save?** Ensure required fields (_Name, Price, Quantity_) are filled in.
- **Sold out?** Increase the quantity if more seats are available.
