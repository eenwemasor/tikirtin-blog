# Managing Attendee Information

Once tickets are sold, you can view and manage your attendee list. This guide explains how to access attendee data, export reports, and communicate with your guests.

---

## 1. Go to Your Event Dashboard

1.  Log in to your Tikirtin account.
2.  Select **Events** from the main menu.
3.  Click on the event you wish to manage.

---

## 2. Access the Attendees Section

1.  From the event dashboard, navigate to the **Attendees** or **Participants** tab.
2.  You will see a list of everyone who has registered for your event.

---

## 3. View and Manage Attendees

In the attendee list, you can:

-   **View Details**: Click on an attendee's name to see their ticket type, contact information, and any custom questions answered during registration.
-   **Search and Filter**: Use the search bar to find a specific person or filter by ticket type.
-   **Check-In Attendees**: If you are using a check-in app, this is where you can mark attendees as arrived.

---

## 4. Export Attendee List

1.  Click the **Export** or **Download** button.
2.  Choose a format (e.g., CSV, Excel).
3.  The file will download to your computer, containing all attendee information for your records or for use with other tools.

---

## ✅ Tips

-   **Communicate Updates**: Use the attendee list to send important event updates via email.
-   **Prepare for Check-In**: Export the list beforehand for a manual check-in process if needed.
-   **Analyze Your Audience**: Use the data to understand who is attending your event for future marketing efforts.

---

## 🚨 Troubleshooting

-   **Attendee not on the list?** Confirm their payment was successful and that they completed the registration process.
-   **Export not working?** Disable any pop-up blockers in your browser and try again.
-   **Incorrect information?** You can often edit attendee details manually, or you can ask the attendee to update their information through their own account.
