# Managing Your Account

Keep your account information up-to-date to ensure you receive important notifications and maintain security. This guide covers how to change your email, password, and other personal settings.

---

## 1. Go to Your Account Settings

1.  Log in to your Tikirtin account.
2.  Click on your profile icon in the top right corner.
3.  Select **Account Settings** from the dropdown menu.

---

## 2. Update Your Personal Information

In the **Profile** or **Personal Info** section, you can update:

-   **Name**: Your first and last name.
-   **Email Address**: The email you use to log in and receive notifications. You may need to verify the new email address.
-   **Profile Picture**: Your personal avatar.

---

## 3. Change Your Password

1.  Navigate to the **Security** or **Password** tab.
2.  Enter your current password for verification.
3.  Enter your new password and confirm it.
4.  Click **Save Changes** or **Update Password**.

---

## 4. Manage Notifications

1.  Go to the **Notifications** tab.
2.  Choose which emails you want to receive, such as newsletters, event reminders, and sales summaries.
3.  Save your preferences.

---

## ✅ Tips

-   **Use a Strong Password**: Combine uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols for better security.
-   **Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**: If available, enable 2FA for an extra layer of security.
-   **Keep Your Email Current**: Ensure your email is up-to-date so you don't miss important information about your events or account.

---

## 🚨 Troubleshooting

-   **Forgot your password?** Use the "Forgot Password?" link on the login page to reset it.
-   **Didn't receive a verification email?** Check your spam or junk folder and make sure you entered the correct email address.
-   **Can't change your email?** Some systems may require you to contact support for this change.

---
