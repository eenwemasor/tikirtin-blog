If you're selling one ticket type at one price for every event, you're leaving money on the table. Full stop.

Tiered pricing - the practice of offering multiple ticket types at different price points - is one of the simplest, most proven ways to increase your revenue, create urgency, and make your event accessible to a wider audience at the same time.

The Early Bird buyer feels rewarded. The VIP buyer feels exclusive. The General Admission buyer gets in. Everyone wins.

In this guide, we'll walk through *why* tiered pricing works and *exactly* how to set it up on Tikirtin, using real screenshots from the platform.


## Why Tiered Pricing Works

Before we get into the how, let's quickly cover the why - because understanding the psychology behind it will help you structure your tiers more effectively.

**It creates urgency.** When someone sees "Early Bird - 30 left", their brain shifts from "should I go?" to "I need to grab this now." Scarcity and time pressure are the two most powerful drivers of ticket sales.

**It maximizes revenue per buyer.** Not everyone who wants to attend your event is price-sensitive. Some people *want* the premium experience - they just need the option. If you only offer one price, your VIP buyers pay the same as everyone else.

**It broadens your audience.** A lower-priced tier (or even a free registration) removes the barrier for people who are interested but hesitant. They're in the room, they're building your community, and they'll buy VIP next time.

**It gives you cash flow earlier.** Early Bird tickets sold weeks before the event put money in your account now - which you can use to fund the event itself.

## Step 1: Create Your First Ticket

From your event dashboard, navigate to the **Add Tickets** step. You'll see an empty ticket list when you start.

<Image width={1400} height={300} src="/content/how-to-set-up-tiered-pricing-for-your-event-tickets/event-ticket-index.png" alt="Event Tickets page showing an empty list and the Add new ticket button" />

Click **+ Add new ticket** in the top-right corner to open the ticket creation form.


## Step 2: Name Your Ticket and Add a Description

Give your ticket a clear name - something attendees will immediately understand, like *Early Bird*, *General Admission*, or *VIP Pass*.

You can also assign it to a **Ticket Section** if you want to group multiple tiers under a shared capacity (more on that below). Add a short description of what's included - this shows during checkout and helps buyers make their decision.

<Image width={1400} height={500} src="/content/how-to-set-up-tiered-pricing-for-your-event-tickets/event-ticket-form-basic.png" alt="Add new ticket form showing Ticket Title, Ticket Section, and Description fields" />


## Step 3: Set the Ticket Type and Price

This is where your tier starts to take shape. Click the **Ticket Type** dropdown. You'll see three options: **Free**, **Paid**, and **Donation**.

<Image width={1400} height={500} src="/content/how-to-set-up-tiered-pricing-for-your-event-tickets/event-ticket-type.png" alt="Ticket Type dropdown showing Free, Paid, and Donation options" />

For most tiers, you'll select **Paid**. Once selected, a **Price** field appears - enter the ticket price in naira (or whichever currency you've set for the event).

You'll also see two important checkboxes:

- **Absorb fees** - When checked, Tikirtin's service fees come out of your revenue instead of being added on top of the ticket price. The buyer pays exactly what you advertised.
- **Absorb VAT fees** - Same idea, but for VAT. Check this if you want to display a clean, all-inclusive price to your buyers.

## Understanding What You Actually Earn Per Ticket

Tikirtin gives you full visibility into your payout before you publish. After entering your price, you can view the full **Ticket Cost Breakdown** - no surprises, no hidden numbers.

<Image width={1200} height={800} src="/content/how-to-set-up-tiered-pricing-for-your-event-tickets/event-ticket-cost-breakdown.png" alt="Ticket Cost Breakdown showing ticket price, service fee, processing fee, VAT, cost to buyer, and payout amount" />

For example, on a NGN 1,000.00 ticket (with fees not absorbed):
- **Service fee:** 5% + NGN 200.00 = NGN 250.00
- **Processing fee:** 2.9% per order = NGN 29.00
- **VAT:** 7.5% per order = NGN 69.77
- **Cost to buyer:** NGN 1,000.00
- **Your payout:** NGN 721.00

Use this breakdown when setting your prices so you know exactly what you'll pocket per tier. If you want the buyer to pay NGN 1,000 clean with no add-ons, tick **Absorb fees** and **Absorb VAT fees** and price accordingly.


## Step 4: Set Quantity and Sales Window

For each tier, set:

- **Quantity** - How many of this ticket type are available. Keep Early Bird quantities low to create urgency (e.g. 30–50 tickets). VIP can be even smaller.
- **Tickets allowed per order** - Minimum and maximum a single buyer can purchase. Default is 1–15.
- **Sales Start Period** - When this ticket goes on sale. Use this to stagger your tiers - Early Bird opens first, then General Admission opens after Early Bird sells out or expires.
- **Sales End Period** - When this ticket stops being available.

This is the key to making tiered pricing *automatic*. Set your Early Bird to close two weeks before the event, and your General Admission to open right after. The system handles the switchover - you don't have to manually update anything.


## Step 5: Set Visibility and Sales Channel

Under the ticket settings, you control exactly how and where this ticket is sold.

<Image width={1200} height={400} src="/content/how-to-set-up-tiered-pricing-for-your-event-tickets/event-ticket-config.png" alt="Sales Channel, Enable Ticket, Make Ticket Visible, and Display Remaining Tickets settings" />

**Sales Channel** - Choose where this ticket can be purchased:

<Image width={1200} height={400} src="/content/how-to-set-up-tiered-pricing-for-your-event-tickets/event-ticket-type-sales-channel.png" alt="Sales Channel dropdown showing Both Online and Door, Online Only, and Event Venue Only" />

- **Online Only** - Sold exclusively through your Tikirtin event page
- **Both Online & Door** - Available online and at the venue on the day
- **Event Venue Only** - Walk-in purchases only (useful for a door ticket tier)

**Make Ticket Visible** - Keep this checked for public tiers. Uncheck it for private or invite-only ticket types (e.g. a staff or sponsor ticket that shouldn't appear on your public event page).

**Display Remaining Tickets** - Turn this on for your Early Bird and VIP tiers. Showing "12 tickets left" is one of the most effective urgency triggers you have - use it.


## Step 6: Use Sections to Manage Shared Capacity

Here's a feature most organizers don't know about - and it's genuinely useful.

If you want multiple ticket types to share a single total capacity, use **Ticket Sections**. For example, say your venue holds 300 people standing. You want to sell both a *Standard Standing* ticket at ₦5,000 and a *Premium Standing* ticket at ₦10,000 - but the total across both can't exceed 300.

Create a section called "Standing" with a capacity of 300, then assign both ticket types to it. Tikirtin will automatically enforce the combined cap.

## Step 7: Repeat for Each Tier

Go back to your ticket list and click **+ Add new ticket** again for each tier in your structure. Each one gets its own name, price, quantity, sales window, and settings. Done right, your ticket page will look something like this:

- **Early Bird** - ₦5,000 | 50 tickets | Closes in 14 days | *12 remaining*
- **General Admission** - ₦8,000 | 200 tickets | On sale now
- **VIP** - ₦20,000 | 30 tickets | *8 remaining*
- **VVIP Table** - ₦50,000 | 10 tickets | *3 remaining*

That's a ticket page that sells.


## A Few Pro Tips on Tiered Pricing

**Don't make tiers too similar.** If your General Admission is ₦8,000 and your VIP is ₦9,000, no one will buy GA. Make the VIP experience clearly worth the premium - exclusive lounge, priority entry, branded gift, reserved seating.

**Use urgency honestly.** Only display "Limited tickets remaining" if it's actually true. Nigerian audiences are savvy - if you fake scarcity, it erodes trust.

**Consider a free tier for corporate or networking events.** A free registration tier for a business conference collects attendee data, gets people in the room, and grows your email list - even if the premium tiers are paid.

**Stagger your launches.** Announce Early Bird first, let the FOMO build, then open General Admission to the people who missed out. It keeps your event in the conversation longer.
